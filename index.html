Biography

Muhammad Lawal Gwaram is a Nigerian student leader and administrator at Federal University Dutse (FUD). He serves as Deputy Chief of Staff (Administration) to the Speaker of the Students’ Representative Assembly, Students’ Union Government, Federal University Dutse (SRA-SUG FUD).

His interests include institutional administration, student representation, research, leadership development, youth empowerment and entrepreneurship. Through student leadership and institutional initiatives, he aims to support effective administration, responsible service, innovation and opportunities for young people.

His earlier student leadership includes serving as Interact President at the School for the Gifted, Gwagwalada. His current work at Federal University Dutse builds on his continuing interest in student representation, administration and public service.
